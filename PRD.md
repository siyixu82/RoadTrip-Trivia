# RoadTrip Trivia — Product Requirements

This project's product requirements live in two documents:

- **[MVP PRD](docs/PRD-MVP.md)** — the current, authoritative scope being built first:
  pre-generated U.S. National Park quizzes (20 questions each), a recommended main
  page, the quiz experience, a completed-history page with scores, and saved &
  downloaded quizzes for offline play.
- **[V1 PRD](docs/PRD-V1.md)** — the full post-MVP product vision: AI quiz generation,
  location awareness, Read-Aloud / Driver Mode, the Explore marketplace, gamified
  results, and the Settings/Profile screen.

Start with the **[MVP PRD](docs/PRD-MVP.md)** for what's being built first.
